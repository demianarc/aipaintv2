The AI Museum is a small experiment: pull a painting at random from one of the world's great collections, then ask an AI to read it the way a thoughtful art historian would.

How it works

Each painting is pulled live from the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection — only public-domain works are shown. The image and metadata go to a multimodal model that looks at the painting and writes a short interpretation: what's there, and what it might mean.

Why

Most of us walk past art without context. The plaque is small, the wall text is dry, the audio guide costs extra. This is an attempt to put the curator's whisper directly next to the painting, for free, on any device.

Privacy

Favorites are kept in your browser's local storage. There's no account, no tracking, no server-side record of what you've saved.
